1. Provides a broad understanding of the Computing Industry

2. Introduces the Computing Profession

Develops awareness of roles, responsibilities, and ethics expected from computing professionals.

3. Shows real-world applications of computing

5. Provides understanding of ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) requirements

Ensures students know the global standards and competencies needed in the computing profession.
