Basic Fighting Stance

The fighting stance is the foundation of Filipino Martial Arts such as Arnis, Eskrima, and Kali. A proper stance improves balance, mobility, and striking power.

  • Stand with feet shoulder width apart
  • Dominant foot slightly behind
  • Knees slightly bent
  • Stick hand forward
  • Non-weapon hand protects the body

Basic Grip

The grip determines control and power of your strikes. In Filipino Martial Arts, the stick is usually held near the base to maximize reach and leverage.

  • Hold the stick about one fist away from the bottom
  • Keep grip firm but relaxed
  • Thumb wrapped around the stick
  • Allow wrist flexibility for faster strikes

12 Striking Techniques

1–4 Diagonal Strikes

Powerful forehand and backhand diagonal attacks aimed at the head or shoulder.

5–6 Horizontal Strikes

Side-to-side strikes targeting the ribs or torso.

7–8 Diagonal Downward Strikes

Reverse diagonal strikes delivered downward.

9–10 Thrusting Attacks

Forward thrust strikes aimed at the stomach or chest.

11 Vertical Strike

A downward strike aimed at the head.

12 Upward Strike

An upward strike from below aimed at the chin or hands.

Basic Blocking Techniques

Blocking techniques are used to defend against incoming strikes. Good defense positions the weapon to intercept attacks while preparing for counterattacks.

  • Inside Block
  • Outside Block
  • High Block
  • Low Block
  • Roof Block

Basic Footwork

Footwork allows a fighter to control distance, avoid attacks, and create angles. Proper movement is essential in Filipino Martial Arts.

  • Forward Step
  • Backward Step
  • Side Step
  • Triangle Step (Male and Female Triangle)
